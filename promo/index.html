Helpful answers

Before you begin

Important details for students and families.

Is this an official NHPC or DHA website?

No. HL Preparation Nepal is an independent educational practice platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NHPC, DHA or any government authority.

Does the app guarantee that I will pass?

No. We do not promise exam results. Use the app for practice and revision alongside the latest official syllabus, guidelines and notices.

Are all faculties and features already complete?

The platform is being developed in stages. Availability can vary by faculty and module, so please check the app itself for the current content.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, the app is designed for mobile browsers and can be added to your home screen on supported devices.

Is AI Tutor available now?

AI Tutor is a planned feature. This promotional website does not provide live AI chat at this time.

How should I use account or membership features?

Use your own account details and do not share your password. If a login or membership issue occurs, use the support option available inside the app. Never submit sensitive personal or payment information in public comments.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

A quick guide for new students.

Is HL Preparation an official NHPC website?

No. It is an independent educational practice resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NHPC.

Does the app guarantee that I will pass?

No study app can guarantee an exam result. Use it for practice and revision alongside the latest official syllabus and notices.

Can I study on my mobile phone?

Yes. The app is designed to work on mobile browsers and can be added to your home screen where supported.

Do I need to create an account?

Some study features may be available without login. An account may be needed for cloud progress sync or membership features.

Where can I send feedback?

Use the app’s available support/contact option. An official Facebook Page link will be added after the Page is created.
